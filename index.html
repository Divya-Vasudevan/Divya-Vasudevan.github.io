Our Courses

Abacus

This is a highly structured, time tested and globally accepted programme in many countries. It activates the brain, enhances calculation speed and accuracy. Abacus developed on "Zhusuan Methodology" originated in China.

The course has 10 levels.

Eligibility: LKG TO STD 8

Vedic Math

Vedic Math is a unique system based on simple rules and principles called sutras which enable mathematical problems to be solved easily and efficiently. Verification of calculation can be done with speed and accuracy. Vedic Math was discovered by The Shankaracharya of Puri in the Vedas as Ganitha Sutras. The aim of Vedic math is to present Mathematics as a unified body of knowledge and reduce the burden on the students.

Eligibility: STD 4 TO STD 9

What to Bring

Students should bring 4 pencils, abacus & hall ticket. Rubber, sharpener, water bottle, writing pads, bags & any other material will not be allowed inside the hall.

Practice

Practice for the competition will be given at no extra cost.

Student should practice for the completed level. If the student is currently doing 5th level, he/she should practice for 4th level and appear for 4th level in the competition.

Each practice paper should be done for 8 minutes. Number of correct sums done in 8 minutes by the student will decide the winner.

Results

The results are announced for each level separately.

Addition & subtraction sums will carry two marks and multiplication & division sums will carry one mark.
