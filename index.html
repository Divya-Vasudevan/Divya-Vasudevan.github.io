About

I thrive on
unraveling complexity.

I'm a product designer based in Bangalore with a Master's in Interaction Design from CCS, Detroit. My experience has largely been with early-stage startups, where I've designed products from the ground up.

Currently, I'm at Incubyte, where I craft thoughtful, user-centric design solutions for clients while also playing an active role in growing and mentoring the design team.

Outside of work, you can often find me lost in a novel, drawn to characters and worlds that stay with me long after the final page.

Design Philosophy.

  • 01 Always be learning

    Design evolves, and so should we.

  • 02 Simplicity is key

    I aim to make complex things feel effortless.

  • 03 Adapt as needed

    I believe in flexible thinking over following rigid blueprints.

  • 04 Design together, not alone

    I believe the best ideas emerge through collaboration and inviting feedback.

  • 05 Understand First

    No design before the problem and constraints are clear.

  • 06 Iterate Until It's Right

    I stay close through implementation, track what works and what doesn't.
